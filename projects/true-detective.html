05 — What I Learned

WHAT I'M TAKING FORWARD.

What stood out

Narrative-based visuals was the most important work on this project. Being able to represent first-season compositing was a big portion of the workload and a major lesson I took away from it — enhancing the grittiness of the visuals from my 3D renderings.

What I'd change

Looking at this project in hindsight, I think it has some room to improve. The music is constricted because I had to use a royalty-free song. Music is an important choice in a title sequence, and I would have liked it to accompany my piece with more distinction.
